Controls & Navigation Guide

Shortcuts, mouse navigation, live editing, and sync features

Block Navigation

↑ ↓
Step Blocks: Jump forward or backward one synchronized block at a time.
Wheel
Mouse Wheel: 1 mouse wheel notch moves down or up exactly 1 block without skipping.
Click
Direct Selection: Click any block in either English or French preview to instantly jump and align.
HUD
Block Stepper / Jump: Click the "Block X/Y" badge in the bottom bar to type a number and jump directly.

Sync & Offset Control

Alt
Pause Sync: Hold Alt while scrolling or clicking to freely navigate one pane independently without moving the other.
[ ]
Nudge Alignment: Shift the French pane's alignment offset by -1 or +1 block if paragraphs are misaligned.
Auto-Sync
Toggle Synchronization: Enable or disable dual-pane locked scrolling from the view control bar.
Reset
Reset Sync: Clears any manual offset and re-synchronizes the active block to parity.
